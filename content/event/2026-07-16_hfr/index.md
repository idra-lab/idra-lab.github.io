We are proud to host the **19th International Workshop on Human-Friendly Robotics (HFR 2026)** at the University of Trento on July 16–17, 2026.

HFR 2026 brings together researchers and industry professionals working on collaborative and human-centered robotics, with a focus on safe, adaptive, and effective human-robot interaction.

## Workshop Overview

- **Scientific Program**: The workshop features peer-reviewed paper presentations, invited keynote talks, and poster sessions covering topics such as physical and cognitive human-robot interaction, shared control, learning by demonstration, benchmarking and evaluation, and ethical aspects of human-robot systems.

- **Interaction & Networking**: HFR fosters close interaction between academia and industry, offering a focused and highly interactive environment for discussion and collaboration.

## Topics of Interest

The workshop addresses a broad spectrum of themes in human-friendly robotics, including:

- Physical and cognitive human-robot interaction  
- Collaborative and social robots  
- Shared control and sliding autonomy  
- Human-in-the-loop systems  
- Control methods for safe and adaptive interaction  
- Human monitoring and intention recognition  
- Benchmarking, performance evaluation, and ethics in HRI  
- Application domains such as medical and rehabilitation robotics, manufacturing, and social robotics  

Join us in Trento to explore the latest advances in human-centered robotics and contribute to shaping the future of safe and intelligent human-robot collaboration.

For more details, visit the official workshop website: https://sites.google.com/unitn.it/hfr2026/
