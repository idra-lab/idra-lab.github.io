IDRA-Lab is pleased to announce its participation in **I-RIM 3D 2025** and **Maker Faire Rome**, held at the **Gazometro area in Rome** from **17 to 19 October 2025**.  
Throughout the event, IDRA researchers will be involved in scientific sessions, workshops, and exhibition activities.

## Exhibition Stands
IDRA will participate with **two exhibition stands**:

- **RELEARN**: showcasing research projects coordinated by IDRA, including the EU-funded projects **[MAGICIAN](https://www.magician-project.eu/)** and **[INVERSE](https://www.inverse-project.org/)**, with a focus on manufacturing, Industry 5.0, and human-centered robotics.

- **ALPINE**: presenting the development of a **climbing robot** for operation in challenging environments, highlighting advanced locomotion and control solutions.

## Workshop
On **17 October 2025**, IDRA will organize the workshop  
**“Industry 5.0: Workplace Transformation with Next Generation Smart Robots”**, focusing on human–robot collaboration and the deployment of collaborative robots in real industrial environments.

## Scientific Contributions
IDRA members will also contribute to **robotics workshops and paper presentations**, covering topics such as autonomous exploration, perception, control, and industrial robotics.

We invite attendees to visit our stands, attend the workshop, and engage with our researchers throughout the event.

For more information on our research activities, visit our [publications page](/publication/).
