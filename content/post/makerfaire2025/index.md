## IDRA Exhibition Activities at I-RIM 3D and Maker Faire 2025
During **I-RIM 3D 2025** and **Maker Faire Rome**, IDRA participated with **two exhibition stands**, presenting ongoing research projects and advanced robotic systems to a broad audience of researchers, practitioners, and the general public.

### RELEARN Stand
The **RELEARN** stand was dedicated to research projects coordinated by IDRA, including the EU-funded projects **[MAGICIAN](https://www.magician-project.eu/)** and **[INVERSE](https://www.inverse-project.org/)**.  
The focus was on robotics for **manufacturing and Industry 5.0**, highlighting intelligent automation, adaptability, and human-centered production systems.

Visitors had the opportunity to learn about ongoing research activities and discuss future directions in industrial robotics.

### ALPINE Stand
The **ALPINE** stand showcased the development of a **climbing robot**, with a particular emphasis on the work led by **Michele Focchi**.

The exhibit demonstrated advanced locomotion and control capabilities designed for operation in challenging environments, attracting strong interest from both technical and non-technical audiences.

### Outreach and Engagement
Through these exhibition activities, IDRA strengthened its presence at major national robotics events, promoting research dissemination, industrial collaboration, and public engagement in robotics and intelligent machines.
