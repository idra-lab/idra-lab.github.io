## Plenary presentations at ILUS 2025

IDRA attended the [2nd International Lung Ultrasound Symposium 2025 (ILUS)](https://event.unitn.it/ilus2025/), held in Trento (Italy) on the 4-6 June 2025.

<!--more-->

The Medical Robotics team, represented by [Davide Nardi](/author/davide-nardi/) and [Edoardo Lamon](/author/edoardo-lamon/) had two talks (plus a third one co-authored) at the 2nd International Lung Ultrasound Symposium 2025 (ILUS), held in Trento.
Our work focused on teleoperated lung ultrasound, a growing area of interest at the intersection of robotics and medical imaging that faces novel challenges, both for roboticists and clinicians. As part of the event, we delivered two 15-minute presentation highlighting our methodology based on a novel way of generating virtual fixtures from the generation of a digital twin.

The session sparked meaningful discussions and valuable feedback from the community. It was a truly amazing experience to connect with leading researchers and clinicians in the field, and to showcase how robotic teleoperation can expand access to advanced diagnostic tools like lung ultrasound.
